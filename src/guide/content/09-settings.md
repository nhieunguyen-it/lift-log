# Changing targets and settings {#settings}

All of these live on the **Settings** tab.

## Weight unit {#settings-unit}

Switch between **kg** and **lbs** at any time. Data is stored in kg, so nothing is lost. Displayed weights are rounded to a loadable value.

## Week start {#settings-week-start}

**Week starts on** can be Monday, Sunday or Saturday. It decides which sets belong to "this week" in Analyze.

## Weekly volume targets {#settings-targets}

Open **Weekly volume targets** to see every muscle with four numbers: MEV, MAV low, MAV high and MRV. Edit any of them; changes save when you leave a field. The default is shown under each row, and **Reset** restores it.

The values must stay in order: MEV is at most MAV low, which is at most MAV high, which is at most MRV. Otherwise an error appears and the change is not saved.

> [!HEURISTIC]
> Raise targets if you consistently recover well at higher volume, lower them if you are struggling. Defaults suit many people but not everyone.

## My equipment {#settings-equipment}

Tick the equipment you have. See Suggestions, alternatives and swaps.

## Profiles {#settings-profiles}

The **Profiles** section shows the current profile and lists all profiles with **Switch**, **Rename** and **Delete**, plus **New profile** and **Import backup into a new profile**. See Profiles.

## Guide and tour {#settings-guide}

The **Guide** card opens this guide. From there you can replay the welcome tour any time.
