# Privacy and disclaimer {#privacy}

## Your data stays with you {#privacy-data}

Lift Log has no account, no server and no analytics. Your workouts, settings and custom exercises are stored in your browser on this device. They are only shared if you export a file and send it somewhere yourself. Encrypted exports are protected by your passphrase.

## Not medical advice {#privacy-disclaimer}

> [!NOTE]
> Volume landmarks, progression targets, deload suggestions and swaps are general training heuristics, not medical advice. They cannot account for injuries, health conditions or medication. Stop if something hurts, and talk to a doctor or qualified coach if you are unsure.

## Credits {#privacy-credits}

Exercise photos are public domain, from the free-exercise-db project. Volume landmark terms (MEV, MAV, MRV) come from the training community, and the defaults here are only starting points.
