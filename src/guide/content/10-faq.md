# FAQ and troubleshooting {#faq}

## Why didn't my set count? {#faq-set-not-counted}

Check that the set is:

- ticked **Done**,
- not marked **Warm-up**,
- logged with at least one rep,
- recorded with RIR no higher than {{maxCountedRir}}, or no RIR.

Also check the week: a workout counts toward the week it started in, and your week-start setting decides where weeks begin.

## Why is a muscle "over"? {#faq-over}

Exercises count for more than one muscle. A lot of pressing can push triceps and front delts above their ceiling even if you never trained them directly. Tap the muscle to see which exercises contributed. If it looks right but feels fine, you can raise that muscle's MRV in Settings.

## Why is a muscle "below" when I trained it? {#faq-below}

Helper muscles only get a share of each set, so a few sets of one exercise may add up to less than the minimum. Hard sets also need a low enough RIR to count.

## The chip says there is no history {#faq-no-history}

Targets look at your previous finished sessions of the same exercise. A swapped or newly created exercise starts fresh.

## What happens if I clear browser data? {#faq-clear-data}

Your log is deleted with it, because it is stored in the browser. Restore from a backup if you have one. Uninstalling the home-screen app on some phones also clears its data, so export first.

## iOS install quirks {#faq-ios}

- Use Safari to add the app to your Home Screen.
- The installed app and the Safari tab can have **separate storage**, so a log made in one may not appear in the other. Pick one and use it, and move data with a backup.
- iOS can clear data of sites you have not opened for a long time. Back up regularly.

## The app looks out of date {#faq-update}

Close and reopen it. When a new version is available it updates after a restart.

## The target seems too easy or hard {#faq-targets}

Targets are conservative double-progression suggestions. You are in charge: type any weight and reps you like.
