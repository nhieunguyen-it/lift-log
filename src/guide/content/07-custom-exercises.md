# Custom exercises {#custom-exercises}

## Create one {#custom-exercises-create}

On the **Exercises** tab press **New exercise**. Fill in:

- **Name**, **Equipment**, **Force** (push, pull, legs, static) and **Mechanic** (compound or isolation).
- **Bodyweight**, if the weight you log is added load.
- An optional **Rep range**. Leave it empty to use the default for the exercise type.
- **Muscles trained**, with a share for each.

Press **Create exercise**. Custom exercises carry a badge, and the **Custom** filter lists only them. Use the pencil button to edit one.

![New exercise dialog with muscle shares](/guide/custom.png)

## How muscle weights work {#custom-exercises-weights}

Choose **exactly one primary muscle at full weight (100%)**. Add helpers at three quarters, half or one quarter. These weights are exactly what Analyze uses: a set of your exercise adds that share of a weighted set to each muscle.

> [!TIP]
> Ask "how much does this muscle work compared with the main one?". A pressing movement might be chest at full weight, triceps at half and front delts at half, just like bench press.

Built-in exercises show their shares as percentages on the Exercises list.

## Editing and deleting {#custom-exercises-edit}

Changing muscle shares changes how its sets are counted in Analyze. Deleting a custom exercise removes it from the list, but workouts that used it keep it and it still counts toward your volume. You are told how many sets are affected before you confirm.
