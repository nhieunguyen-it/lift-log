# Suggestions, alternatives and swaps {#suggestions}

## Volume suggestions {#suggestions-volume}

On Analyze, **This week's suggestions** lists muscles worth a change:

- **Add sets:** a muscle below its minimum effective volume gets "add N sets" and up to {{volumeSuggestions.exercisesPerMuscle}} exercises that would train it.
- **Over the ceiling:** a muscle above MRV gets a warning to consider easing off.

Tap a suggestion to open that muscle's details. Only the first few show; press **Show more** for the rest. If nothing needs changing it says so.

> [!HEURISTIC]
> Suggestions compare your week to the volume landmarks. They do not know about injuries, soreness or your goals, so treat them as prompts.

## Alternatives and swaps {#suggestions-swaps}

Press the swap button on an exercise card to see **similar exercises for your equipment**. They are ranked by how closely their muscle weights match, with a nudge for the same movement pattern and for exercises you have not already done this week. Each row shows a match score and a "done this week" tag where it applies.

Tap one to swap. The sets you already entered are kept, only the exercise changes.

## My equipment {#suggestions-equipment}

In Settings, **My equipment** is a list of checkboxes (barbell, dumbbell, machine and so on). Suggestions, alternatives and swaps only offer exercises you can do with what is ticked. Bodyweight is always on. Program exercises that need something else get a **Not in your equipment** flag.

> [!TIP]
> If the alternatives list says nothing was found, tick more equipment in Settings and try again.
